# Privacy

<p class="updated">Effective and last updated: 17 October 2026</p>

<div class="note">
  <p>Bryo Studio is a one-person software studio. We collect as little as we can, never ask for an account, show no ads, and never sell your data or share it for advertising.</p>
</div>

## Each product’s policy

Each app and tool has its own page saying exactly what it records and why:

- [Pizza Ambulance privacy policy](/privacy/pizza-ambulance/)
- [Ascribe privacy policy](/privacy/ascribe/): it collects nothing

## What applies to all our apps

- No accounts, no ads, no advertising ids, and no third-party advertising or analytics code.
- An app only records what it needs to work and to be improved, and its own policy lists it.
- Data that leaves your phone is sent encrypted (HTTPS), is tied to a random id rather than to you, and is kept for a limited time.
- We use service providers only to run our servers and store data, and only on our instructions. We do not sell data or share it for advertising.
- Our apps are not directed at children under 13.
- You can ask us to delete your data, or for a copy, by email.

## This website

This website is plain pages: no cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no scripts. It is hosted by **Netlify**, whose servers, like any web server, see your IP address and browser details when you visit, and keep short-lived logs for running the service and keeping it secure. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

## If you email us

If you write to us, we keep your email and our reply to answer you and to deal with what you asked. We do not add you to any mailing list. Ask us and we will delete the conversation, unless we must keep it to handle your request.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your data, such as to see it, correct it, have it deleted or object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Write to us and we will answer within 30 days.

## Changes

We will update this page and its date before any change takes effect.
