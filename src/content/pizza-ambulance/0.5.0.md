0.5.0 brings the town to life. There are cars on the roads and people on the pavements in both parts of town, and a scooter at the bike shop for when you want to go faster than the bike will take you.

## New

- **The scooter**, $4,500 at the bike shop, on its own tab. It's faster than the bike on the road, but it turns wider, feels bumps more and can't hop.
- **The handbrake.** On the scooter, the hop button becomes a blue **STEADY** button. Hold it and the scooter slows and the stack stops swinging, settling instead of sliding. It costs you speed, not slices. You'll hear the scooter's engine rise as you speed up.
- **Choose your ride at the pizzeria.** The vehicles you own are parked beside the bay. Tap one to ride it, and the game remembers your choice next time.
- **Bike-only places.** Bollards and a "bikes only" sign close the alleys and the flyover's ramp to the scooter. A job that needs the hop, such as Five over the Jump, says "Bike only" on its card while you're on the scooter.
- **Cars on the roads.** Both parts of town have traffic. The cars keep left, give way at junctions, stop for the drawbridge, show their brake lights and indicators, and honk. Running into one during a delivery hits the stack like a wall and costs $10 in damages, taken off that delivery's pay. Bumping one while you're riding around between jobs is free.
- **People on the pavements.** They walk, cross when the road is clear, and jump out of the way at the last moment if you're coming fast. Knocking one during a delivery shakes the stack like a scrape and costs $5. Nobody gets hurt.
- **Damages on the results**: a small red line shows what the delivery's bumps cost you, before the pay is added up.

## Changed

- Some medals in the Harbour are a little easier, since the cars and people now slow everyone down: the Harbour Tour and eight of its bank jobs.
