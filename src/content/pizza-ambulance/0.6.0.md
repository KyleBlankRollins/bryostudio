0.6.0 takes the money out of Pizza Ambulance. Everything you can own is now earned with medals or given by a job, and the clock you race is the medals themselves.

## New

- **Everything is earned.** There are no prices and no pay. Your medal total opens the next thing, and the result card shows how many medals the next reward needs. The Harbour opens at 8 medals and the scooter at 45.
- **The medal clock.** The heat countdown is gone. The clock on screen counts down to gold, then silver. Running out never ends the ride, and delivering every stop is always worth bronze. Every job's medals were reset to match.
- **Pet rescues.** Each pet now comes from a job that rescues it: four on Hillside's jobs, and the dog, parrot, penguin and komodo in the Harbour. A pet's upgrades grow with the slices it rescues for you. You can name your pets at the kennel.
- **The scooter, made worth having.** Its STEADY button now carves, settling the stack through corners, surges and hard stops. It has an alley of its own in Hillside, and Express jobs only the scooter can take. Ride it straight out of the bike shop. Cars and people notice it sooner.
- **Where the slices went.** After a job, the result card says what cost you slices and gives one tip.
- **Relaxed.** Easy is now called Relaxed. If you keep missing a medal, the game may suggest it, and you can say no.
- **A calmer job board.** It opens on three jobs up next, folds the rest away, and opens new jobs a few at a time.

## Changed

- Hillside has 4 cars instead of 6, and hitting a car or a person costs slices, not money.
- The kennel, the bike shop and the bus stops now sit off the road.
- People on the pavements no longer pop in and out of view.

Your medals, jobs and everything you own are kept. Only the money is gone.
