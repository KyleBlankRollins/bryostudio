0.4.2 is mostly about having more to do once you've finished the jobs on the board. Each part of town now has a bank of new jobs that open as you win medals, and a special job with a hat for its gold. The racks are gone: the bike carries any number of boxes, and the money goes on suspension instead.

## New

- **More jobs.** Hillside and the Harbour each have a bank of new jobs, about 80 in all, under the jobs you already know. They open in tiers: the first tier once every job above it has a medal, and each next tier once you hold half the medals of the tier before. A locked tier shows a padlock and the medals it still needs. The board counts how many of the bank's golds you have.
- **The day's pick.** Each day one bank job in each part of town has a ribbon. It pays the same as any other and never runs out; it's just a suggestion.
- **Special jobs.** Five over the Jump in Hillside and The Old Quay in the Harbour, with a violet ribbon, open with the bank's second tier. Their golds win a hat: the Hard Hat and the Captain's Cap.
- **About 18 new doors** to deliver to across both parts of town, and some new sights in the distance: wind turbines and a water tower over Hillside, and a cargo ship, a buoy and turbines off the Harbour.
- **Steer from anywhere.** Put your thumb down anywhere below the top of the screen to steer. The hop button moves to the corner away from your steering thumb and only shows while you steer. The left-handed setting is gone, since it's no longer needed.
- **The gold beacon.** The companions' top upgrade is now a gold beacon on their back in place of the gold cape. It turns while the job's whole-box rescue is still unused, and goes dark once it's used.

## Changed

- **No more racks.** The bike carries as many boxes as a job needs; your medals decide which jobs you can take. If you owned any racks, you got back what you paid for them ($100, $300 and $1,800), once, when the update first loads.
- **Suspension, in three steps,** at the bike shop:
  - **Sprung platform**, $300: bumps shake the stack less.
  - **Damped platform**, $1,800, after the sprung one: the stack settles sooner after a wobble.
  - **Bike suspension**, $2,500: softer kerbs and landings. If you'd already bought it, you keep it, and it now does this instead of steadying the lean.

## Fixed

- On some Samsung phones with a 120 Hz screen, the game drew twice as many frames as it needed, using more battery for no difference you could see. It now keeps to 60.

## Smoother

- **Easier on your battery.** With battery saver on, or Game Mode set to Battery, the game rides at 30 frames a second and draws the town at a lower resolution.
- While the in-game phone is open, the screen can dim and sleep as it normally would, and a still world stops its physics behind the phone.
