Pizza Ambulance

You are a rider on a bicycle with a tower of pizzas strapped to the back. The town is open, hilly and in a hurry. Keep the pizzas hot. Keep them whole.

What you do

  • Take delivery jobs across town and earn medals for the good ones.
  • Balance a stack of pizza boxes over hills, kerbs and corners. Every bump counts.
  • Ride down to the harbour, where the wind pushes back and the drawbridge can be jumped if you are brave.
  • Spend what you earn on hats and bigger racks, so you can carry more and look good doing it.
